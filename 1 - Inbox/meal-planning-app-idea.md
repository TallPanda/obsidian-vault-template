Idea: a simple meal planning tool with pantry tracking

Core concept: you log what's in your pantry once a week, and the app suggests meals based on what you already have. Reduces food waste, reduces "what should I cook tonight" friction.

Open questions:
- How granular does pantry tracking need to be? (exact quantities vs just "do I have X")
- Is this a web app, mobile app, or just a script + plain text files?
- Does it need recipes pre-loaded or is it just a matching tool?

Might already exist — check before building.
