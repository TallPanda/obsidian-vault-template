# Sourdough Fermentation Basics

---

A sourdough starter is a live culture of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria sustained by regular flour and water feedings.

**Wild yeast** (primarily *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* and *Kazachstania humilis*) produces CO₂ for leavening. **Lactic acid bacteria** produce acids that give sourdough its tang and inhibit spoilage organisms.

Temperature strongly affects the balance between the two:
- Warmer (~26°C) favours yeast activity and faster rise
- Cooler (~18°C) favours bacterial activity and more acidic flavour

A starter at 100% hydration (equal weights flour and water) is the most common starting point and easiest to maintain.
