# Home Cooking — Overview

## Overview

This guide covers the fundamentals of cooking at home: understanding ingredients, basic techniques, and building reliable habits around food preparation.

Home cooking is a skill that compounds — small investments in understanding (why heat does what it does, how flavour develops, when to salt) pay off across every meal you ever make.

## Sections

- [[Fermentation]] — wild yeast, lacto-fermentation, sourdough starters
- More sections added as notes develop

## Key Ideas

- **Mise en place** — prepare and measure everything before you start cooking. Reduces mistakes and stress.
- **Seasoning at each stage** — salt early and often rather than correcting at the end.
- **Heat control** — most cooking mistakes are heat mistakes. Learn to read the pan.

## Where to Start

If you're new to this area, read [[Fermentation]] first as a concrete example of how a single technique can unlock a range of foods (bread, yoghurt, kimchi, pickles).
