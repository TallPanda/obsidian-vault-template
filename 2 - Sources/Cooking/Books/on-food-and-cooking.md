## Notes

### Fermentation and Leavening

> "Sourdough fermentation is one of the oldest food processes known, predating the isolation of commercial yeast by millennia." — p.517

Wild yeast and bacteria work together in a starter culture — yeast provides lift, bacteria provide flavour and preservation.

### Maillard Reaction

The browning of bread crust is a Maillard reaction (amino acids + reducing sugars under heat), not simple caramelisation. Happens above ~140°C and produces hundreds of flavour compounds.

### Salt's Role in Dough

Salt strengthens gluten networks by reducing electrostatic repulsion between protein chains. It also slows fermentation — important for controlling timing.
